Gelatine Sculpt Ingredients

Gelatine Sculpt is marketed as a dietary supplement for weight-management support. Its reported formula includes botanical extracts and other compounds commonly found in wellness supplements. Below are eight ingredients explained in detail. Please verify these ingredients against the product’s current official label, as online descriptions may differ and the individual amounts may not be disclosed.

1

Green Tea Leaf Extract

Green tea leaf extract contains natural plant compounds called catechins, including EGCG. These compounds have been studied for their antioxidant properties and potential effects on energy metabolism. Green tea extract may also contain caffeine, depending on how it is prepared. However, the amount of caffeine and catechins matters, and the presence of green tea extract does not guarantee significant weight loss.
2

African Mango Seed Extract

African mango seed extract is commonly used in supplements marketed for weight management. It has attracted interest because researchers have investigated its possible relationship with body weight and metabolic health. However, available research is limited, and stronger clinical studies are needed to establish its effectiveness. Consumers should not assume that this ingredient automatically reduces body fat or produces lasting results.
3

Guarana Seed Extract

Guarana is a tropical plant whose seeds naturally contain caffeine. Caffeine can temporarily increase alertness and may have modest effects on energy expenditure. Its impact depends on the dose, individual sensitivity, and other caffeine sources in the diet. Too much caffeine can cause sleep disturbances, nervousness, or a rapid heartbeat, so the total amount in a supplement is important.
4

Grape Seed Extract

Grape seed extract is a source of polyphenols, including proanthocyanidins, which have antioxidant properties. These compounds have been studied for their potential effects on several aspects of health. In a supplement formula, grape seed extract may contribute plant-derived compounds, but there is insufficient evidence to conclude that it independently produces meaningful weight loss.
5

Maca Root Extract

Maca root originates from the Andes and has a long history of traditional use. It is included in various dietary supplements intended to support general wellness and vitality. Research has examined several potential effects of maca, but evidence for direct fat-burning or body-contouring benefits is insufficient. Its inclusion should therefore be understood as a botanical component rather than a proven weight-management solution.
6

Capsicum Fruit Extract

Capsicum fruit extract comes from peppers and may contain capsaicinoids, the compounds responsible for their characteristic heat. Researchers have investigated these compounds for their potential effects on thermogenesis and energy expenditure. Any effects are likely to depend on the formulation and dosage. Capsicum extract should not be considered a substitute for a balanced diet or regular exercise.
7

L-Carnitine

L-carnitine is a naturally occurring compound that helps transport long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria, where they can be used for energy production. It is commonly found in sports-nutrition and wellness products. Although it has been studied for body-composition outcomes, results are mixed, and the presence of L-carnitine alone does not establish that Gelatine Sculpt causes fat loss.
8

Chromium Picolinate

Chromium picolinate is a supplemental form of chromium, a trace mineral involved in normal nutrient metabolism. It is frequently included in products marketed for metabolic wellness. However, evidence that chromium supplementation meaningfully reduces body weight is limited. The quantity in Gelatine Sculpt should be checked on the current product label before drawing conclusions about its potential effects.
